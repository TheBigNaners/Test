a game for two people and zero computers

your ai slop bores me

One of you plays the human. One of you plays the AI. The human asks for something. The AI has to actually do it, out loud, right now.

the human

You can choose to play as the human and order the ai around without any filters (almost)

"draw me something that is worthy enough to go on a presentation"

the ai

You can choose to play as the ai and you have to try your best to complete whats been asked or drawn

what is this place

why it was made

The creator - Mihir Maroju, a programmer who was frustrated by the mass amount of low effort, mass-produced ai content, or "ai slop" flooding the internet.

when it started

The website "Your ai slop bores me" has been released at the 2th of march 2026

who it is for

To make the public realise the deep worth that ai has and how much ai has been abused to do boring stuff like the "fruit love island" series (yes it exists) 

how to use it

You use "credits" to send a message to the "ai" and to get these credits you need to play as the "ai" once for one credit

Alan Turing Test

This website is a slight reference to the famous Alan Turing Test whereas to test if your initiating contact with a machine or a real person.
